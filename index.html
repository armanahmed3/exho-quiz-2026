Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are asked in each quiz session?

Every student receives 10 randomized questions based on their selected class and subject.

What happens if the 90 seconds timer ends?

The quiz automatically submits whatever answers have been selected up to that second and forwards the student to the result & voting screen.

Why do I need to log in before accessing the main portal?

Logging in allows the system to save your scores, track your attempts, and authorize your vote for teachers securely.
